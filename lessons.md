# Proposed lessons for the agents

Each line is a change to the agent instructions. Nothing is applied until the owner approves it.

- Do not promise outcomes or timelines the business has not approved. Offer the earliest date after an inspection instead. (caught on FU-L-101)
- Read prices from data/business.json every time you write one. Never reuse a remembered or cached price. (caught on FU-L-102)
- If a lead has no personal name, greet the company or use a neutral hello. Never send a draft with a template field left in it. (caught on FU-L-103)
- Check every proposed visit against business hours before writing it. If a customer says 'weekend', offer Saturday morning. (caught on FU-L-104)
- When a call summary shows a price that differs from the price list, flag it for the owner. Do not repeat it in a follow-up. (caught on CS-C-203)
